## Brand & Style
The design system embodies "Clinical Haute Couture"—an intersection of world-class medical dermatology and refined Middle Eastern hospitality. Tailored for discerning clients in Amman seeking high-precision laser procedures, aesthetic injectables, and medical dermatology, the interface radiates clinical reassurance wrapped in understated luxury. 

The aesthetic marries **Minimalism with Tactile Luxury**:
- Generous, serene negative space in warm alabaster and cream, rejecting sterile medical blues in favor of architectural tranquility.
- Refined micro-interactions with brass luminescence and polished glass-like card overlays.
- A delicate balance between clinical authority (certifications, scientific transparency, board accreditation badges) and five-star spa indulgence.
- Cultural alignment: High visual elegance, intuitive dual-language readiness, and frictionless direct communication channels.

## Colors
The palette balances deep, clinical sovereignty with warming architectural neutrals and radiant metallic accents:

- **Deep Emerald Slate (`#142823`, Primary):** Anchors high-contrast typographic hierarchy, high-tier navigation bars, and definitive interactive states. Provides medical rigor and timeless prestige without the cold detachment of pure black.
- **Warm Champagne Brass (`#C5A059`, Secondary):** Used selectively for key actions, luxury borders, appointment milestones, active tab indicators, and award seal flourishes. A brighter highlight variant (`#DFBD69`) serves as hover illumination.
- **Ivory & Alabaster Canvas (`#FAF8F5`, Neutral Base; `#F5F1EA`, Surface Container):** Replaces harsh standard whites with soft, calming physical-paper warmth that soothes eye strain and communicates elevated concierge service.
- **Direct Concierge Emerald (`#25D366`, Tertiary):** Reserved strictly for high-conversion real-time scheduling, VIP consultations, and WhatsApp desk integration.
- **Soft Rose Gold & Blush (`#E8D5CE`, Accent Surface):** Applied to treatment category tags, post-care notifications, and gentle badge backgrounds.

## Typography
Typographic scale hinges on high editorial contrast:

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved exclusively for structural titles, hero assertions, doctor credentials, and procedure category headings. Titles utilize optical kerning and measured line-heights to invoke classic magazine spreads.
- **Contemporary Humanist Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for clinical body copy, scheduling forms, procedure dossiers, pricing details, and micro-labels. Its open apertures and geometrical balance maintain effortless legibility across dense medical disclosures and small screen interfaces.
- **All-Caps Eyebrows (`label-md`, `label-sm`):** Rendered with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em`–`0.1em`) to frame procedures (e.g., `FDA APPROVED • FRACTIONAL LASER`) with clinical prestige.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an architectural 12-column grid on desktop screens, scaling dynamically to a 6-column model on tablets and 4 columns on mobile devices:

- **Spatial Rhythm:** Spacious padding mimics open, quiet clinical suites. Sections are separated by generous vertical expanses (`space-xl` to twice `space-xl`) to prevent sensory overload.
- **Content Alignment:** Maximum layout width caps at `1280px` for optimal narrative readability and symmetrical framing of doctor profiles and technology galleries.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1024px)`: Full 12-column system, 48px outer margins, 24px gutters.
  - `Tablet (768px – 1023px)`: 6-column system, 32px outer margins, 20px gutters.
  - `Mobile (<= 767px)`: 4-column system, 20px outer margins, 16px gutters with continuous vertical stacking.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids artificial plastic dropshadows, relying instead on ambient warmth and subtle luminosity:

- **Tonal Layering:** Base surfaces (`#FAF8F5`) host elevated panels (`#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCFA`) outlined with ultra-fine, metallic-tinted borders (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.22)`).
- **Ambient Brass Glows:** Floating cards and selected appointment booking triggers employ a warm diffused undertone: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(20, 40, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Glass Frosting for Overlays:** Floating sticky concierge bars and medical dialogue modals use high-diffusion backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.84);`).
- **Laser / Treatment Cards:** Maintain crisp, hairline separation rather than heavy shadows, emphasizing clinical precision.

## Shapes
A unified pill-shaped and organic curvature system establishes approachable calm while softening medical severity:

- **Full Pills (`rounded-full`):** Applied to primary calls-to-action, instant concierge buttons, trust badges, and interactive category pills.
- **Architectural Curves (`rounded-xl`, 24px–32px):** Treatment spotlight modules, before/after comparison viewports, and clinic technology galleries leverage softened structural arches and corners.
- **Clinical Form Inputs:** Feature comfortable 12px to 16px corner radii, preventing aggressive rectangular sharpness while maintaining structural order.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Book Consultation"):** Deep emerald slate background (`#142823`), soft ivory typography, pill geometry (`padding: 14px 28px`), with an interactive state featuring a subtle champagne border glow.
- **Secondary CTA ("Explore Treatments"):** Soft cream surface, bordered by hairline brass (`#C5A059`), transitioning to a soft brass wash on hover.
- **Concierge Instant Action ("WhatsApp Clinic Desk"):** Vibrant emerald (`#25D366`) pill button with crisp white typography, paired with a dual-ring pulse animation for live availability.

### Cards & Service Dossiers
- **Treatment Cards:** Rendered on crisp warm white backgrounds with a delicate 1px border (`#EFE9DF`), featuring an image framed in soft architectural curvature, high-contrast Playfair typography, procedure duration indicators, and an accent badge indicating laser technology (e.g., "Fotona 4D", "Candela Pro").
- **Doctor & Specialist Profiles:** Elegant vertical layout with neutral portrait frames, accreditation subheadings in spaced uppercase, and soft blush skill chips.

### Trust Badges & Certifications
- **Medical Accreditations:** Pill-shaped markers with muted gold or rose gold backgrounds (`#F3EBE7`), featuring gold icons (shield, leaf, medical cross) and crisp `label-sm` typography emphasizing certified laser specialists and Ministry of Health approval.

### Form Inputs & Appointment Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Generous touch targets (48px height), pale neutral fill (`#F5F1EA`), border transitioning from warm stone to champagne gold on focus (`#C5A059`), with floating labels in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- **Date & Time Slots:** Pill-shaped toggle tiles that turn solid `#142823` with gold text when selected, displaying instant real-time doctor availability.

### Selection Chips & Toggles
- **Category Filter Chips:** Horizontal pill lists with smooth inertia scrolling. Inactive chips feature transparent fills with `#E8D5CE` outlines; active chips feature `#142823` solid fill with bright ivory text.
